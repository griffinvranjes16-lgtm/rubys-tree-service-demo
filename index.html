Fall & Storm Season · Tree Safety Check

Before the winter wind finds the weak branch

Once the leaves drop, problems are easier to spot — and winter wind and heavy snow will test every weak limb. If something looks off, now is the time to get it looked at.

  • Dead or hanging limbs over the roof, driveway, or walkway
  • A tree leaning further than it used to, or with cracks in the trunk
  • Brush and storm debris piled up before the snow covers it
  • Stumps left from summer removals, ready to grind before the ground freezes
